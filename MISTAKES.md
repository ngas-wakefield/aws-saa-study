## Patterns to watch

- choosing a technically possible answer instead of the one that best satisfies all requirements
- missing phrases such as **most cost-effective**, **least operational overhead**, **highly available**, **fault tolerant**, **automatically scale**, **decouple**, **durable**, **low latency**, **private**, **real-time**, **minimal changes**
- confusing availability features with performance/scaling features
- over-engineering when a managed service is explicitly favored by the scenario
